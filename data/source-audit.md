## Atmosphere and direction

Warm industrial tavern editorial: railway heritage without kitsch, rust/brick and dark ink with a controlled brass accent. Strong wordmark, generously scaled interiors and comfort-food photography, robust serif/display headline system paired with simple utility labels.

R Street: Interior photo: red brick piers, black tufted leather banquettes, dark bentwood chairs, embossed dark ceiling, cone pendant lamps, broad timber communal tables, and red upper-wall lighting.

Folsom: Exterior photo: two-story rust-red stucco facade with tall dark-framed windows, an arched upper window, white rectangular wordmark sign, deep black covered patio structure, and string lights. Food thumbnails show sociable brunch/cocktails.
