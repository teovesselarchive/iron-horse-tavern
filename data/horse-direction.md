# Iron Horse replacement direction, November 6, 2026

The user rejected the previous cream/condensed-type homepage as bland and generic, and called out the random kimchi headline. This direction replaces that design. Only Iron Horse is in scope.

## Direction contract

Mode: Persuade. A visitor should recognize Iron Horse as a substantial Sacramento tavern, want to spend time there, and immediately find reservations, ordering or private events.

World: the physical tavern sign and the room behind it. Brick red from the R Street walls, warm cream lettering, blackened steel and leather, restrained copper. Heavy Alfa Slab One lettering draws from the muscular slab forms of the existing wordmark and railway-era signage; DM Sans handles practical information. The actual official logo is preserved.

First viewport: the real R Street room fills the frame. “R Street raised us.” makes a specific origin statement. The original location and Palladio are named; Reserve, Order and Events are visible actions. No fabricated station history, random ingredient slogan, highlighted word, stock photograph or agency motif.

Visitor path: room and identity, short local story, interactive real-dish sampler, happy hour and cocktail photograph, asymmetric room gallery, strong private-event invitation, weekend brunch, branch-specific visit details, and complete useful footer.

Signature interaction: on desktop, focus or hover a menu item to change its large photograph. On phones, tapping an item places the photograph directly beneath it. All descriptions remain readable without JavaScript. Location selection displays the matching address, hours, phone, parking and live booking destination. The existing keyboard gallery remains intact.

Motion: one short photographic arrival on the opening room, no continuous scroll hijack or repeated section reveals. Reduced-motion disables it. Persistent content is never hidden for animation.

Cross-surface reach: all 11 HTML pages receive the new navigation, palette, typography and footer; menu and event copy rewritten while preserving current restaurant service destinations.

Quality bar: expressive and restaurant-specific like the approved Café X turnaround, without copying its palette, typeface, hero photo arrangement, community badge or copy. The prior rejected Iron Horse screenshot is the anti-reference. Agency quality depends on a coherent venue identity, real photos, deliberate pacing and correct interactions, not generic luxury language.

Build path: code-led. No approved image comp. The Impeccable launcher was unavailable in this session; no seed, comp-diff or detector success is claimed. User delegated the design decisions and requested execution, so no additional approval questionnaire was added.

## Research actually inspected

- The saved Café X rejection/rebuild conversation, later visual approval, design record and approved screenshots were inspected. Its lesson is a complete client-specific visual world, not the reuse of its slogans or layouts.
- Amrit Palace, CSS Design Awards Website of the Day, October 20, 2025: https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/amrit-palace/48226/ . Visually inspected award hero and catering captures. Kept: room photography as the visual environment and service-oriented paths. Did not take its elegant thin serif, review panel or layout.
- Tripletta Pizza, CSS Design Awards Website of the Day, March 27, 2025: https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/tripletta-pizza/47250 . Visually inspected brand-dominant hero and photographic people spread. Kept: unapologetic brand scale and varied photographic rhythm. Did not reuse its mascot, green palette or labels.
- Cantina del Sol, CSS Design Awards Website of the Day, August 22, 2024: https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/cantina-del-sol/46113/ . Visually inspected type-led hero and restaurant photography section. Kept: total color commitment and confident scale. Did not reuse lime imagery, halftones or rounded food carousel.
- Official Iron Horse site and existing primary-source menus, MAC venue pages, actual Google Maps gallery observations from this project, and all used official photos. Primary architectural context: https://www.vg-architects.com/15th-r . This was adaptive reuse of an office building, so no historic-station claim is made.

Award archive images are research references only and are not bundled as website assets. The archive captures were downloaded and inspected without controlling an external browser. All browser checks for this redesign were local previews.
