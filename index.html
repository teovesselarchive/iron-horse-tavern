Tuna poke nachos, truffle-honey pizza, a Cubano with thick-cut bacon. Iron Horse carries R Street’s railway history into a tavern with a much wider appetite.

Tuna Poke Nachos

Wonton chips carry tuna, avocado, sweet onion and nori, with spicy soy, wasabi cream and sriracha mayonnaise.

Giant Bavarian Pretzel

A large pretzel with sriracha cream cheese and honey mustard.

Cubano

Black Forest ham and thick-cut bacon on a hoagie with Gruyère, pickles, pepperoncini and whole-grain mustard.

Honey Truffle Pizza

Pepperoni and salami with mozzarella, tomato, pickled Fresno chiles and truffle honey.

Stuffed French Toast

Cream-cheese French toast with berries, whipped cream and caramel.

Korean Fried Chicken

Chicken with kimchi, spicy pickled vegetables, jalapeños, rice and sunny-side-up eggs.
